Face Symmetry Improvement Routine

Right side dominant (Yellow sticker side)
Goal: Strengthen LEFT side & relax RIGHT side

Dominant Side Analysis

  • RIGHT side: Slightly dominant (jaw & cheek)
  • LEFT side: Needs more muscle activation
  • Cause: chewing habit, posture, neck tilt, or sleep position

7-Minute Daily Face Symmetry Routine

1. Neck Reset – Foundation

Time: 1 minute

  • Stand straight or against a wall
  • Pull chin straight back (no tilt)
  • Hold 5 seconds → relax

Reps: 10

2. Jaw Centering

Time: 1.5 minutes

  • Tongue lightly on roof of mouth
  • Open mouth slowly (5 sec)
  • Close slowly (5 sec)
  • Keep jaw centered

Reps: 6–8

3. LEFT Cheek Activation

Time: 1.5 minutes

  • Smile gently
  • Place finger on LEFT cheek
  • Lift slightly higher than right
  • Hold 8–10 seconds

Reps: 10 (LEFT side only)

4. Mouth Corner Balance

Time: 1 minute

  • Say “EEE” (stretch lips)
  • Then say “OOO” (pucker)
  • Watch symmetry in mirror

Reps: 15–20 cycles

5. Eye Balance

Time: 1 minute

  • Fingers under eyebrows
  • Close eyes against light resistance
  • Hold 3 seconds → release

Reps: 10

6. Tongue Posture (Mewing – Light)

Time: 1 minute (throughout day)

  • Tongue flat on roof of mouth
  • Teeth lightly touching
  • Lips closed, breathe through nose

Daily Habits to Follow

  • Chew food 70% on LEFT side
  • Avoid jaw clenching (especially right side)
  • Avoid resting chin on hand
  • Reduce phone & laptop neck bending
  • Do light massage on RIGHT jaw at night

Beard & Styling Tips

  • Keep beard slightly fuller on LEFT
  • Sharper jaw line on LEFT side
  • Avoid heavy hair volume only on right
  • Ensure glasses sit evenly on nose
Expected Results:
2 weeks – better muscle control
4–6 weeks – visible symmetry improvement
3 months – stable results with habits
⚠️ If you feel jaw pain, clicking, or discomfort — stop jaw exercises.
Consistency > Force | Gentle & Daily Works Best
